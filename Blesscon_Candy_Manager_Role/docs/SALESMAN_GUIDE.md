# Blesscon Candy — Salesman quick guide

**Sign in:** Use the login supplied by the owner. Open Account to change your temporary password. Use the same phone/browser for pending offline work.

1. **Check your target.** Dashboard and Salesmen & targets show today's effective target, achieved cartons and remaining cartons. Unmet targets carry to the next working day under the owner's settings. Daily tracking shows the calculation.
2. **Create your sale.** Sales orders → New sale. Choose an existing customer or tap New customer. Check your salesman selection, date and retail/wholesale channel.
3. **Enter quantities.** Search a product by ID/name, then enter cartons and loose boxes. Add each product once. The rate changes automatically with total Rani quantity on the invoice. Only the owner can manually override a rate. Watch stock warnings before confirming.
4. **Make quotations.** Quotations → New quotation. Add the customer and products, check the validity date and terms, then save. You can edit Draft/Sent quotations and update their status. Conversion checks stock and creates one order; do not enter the same sale a second time.
5. **Record collections if authorised.** Invoices & payments → Payment. Enter the exact amount, date, method and reference. The receipt is allocated to that invoice. Recovery against an older invoice appears separately from today's sales. If collection is unavailable, ask the owner to grant access. Never record money before collecting it.
6. **Check stock and returns.** Stock & expiry shows warehouse/salesman balances and movement history permitted for your account. Record a return with its condition and quantity; damaged, expired or unsaleable stock is not saleable inventory. Ask management/warehouse staff to issue stock before relying on salesman-held balances.
7. **Submit your closing.** Daily closing shows cash and stock expected from recorded transactions. Enter actual counted cash and every product count. Explain any difference, then submit once; management approval locks the day. Contact the owner if a transaction must be corrected after submission.
8. **Provide the document.** View → PDF/Print. Share PDF can attach the file on supported phones. WhatsApp and Email open a message; attach the downloaded PDF yourself when required. Payment status and balance appear automatically.

**Offline:** The badge shows pending changes. These are saved on your device and retried when internet returns. Stock and prices are checked again by the server. Pending documents are provisional; final PDF/share waits for sync. If a conflict appears, do not repeat the sale. Contact the owner with the displayed message and export the pending change if asked.

**Access:** You can see your own orders, quotations, customers and targets. Owner settings, other salesmen's records and deletion actions are restricted by the database.

**End of day:** Sync, check that your sales and receipts are listed correctly, and review any remaining target or payment balance with the owner.
